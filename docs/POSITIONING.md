# Positioning: Dobara, the retention agent for consumer brands

Oct 25, 2026. Built to clear two bars: stay off Bolna's template turf, and win on market potential, which the organiser says is judged most.

---

## 1. What Bolna already ships, and what we must not be

Bolna's agent library has 15 templates: customer support, cart abandonment, COD confirmation, announcements, reminders, credit-card sales, loan sales, front desk, salon booking, dentist appointment, surveys, recruitment, lead qualification, onboarding, property tech, weekend planner. Every one is a single script a brand triggers once.

**None of them is replenishment, win-back or retention.** The two nearest, cart abandonment (before purchase) and reminders (EMI collections), stop at the sale or the debt. Our product starts after delivery and never stops.

Rules so the judges never think "template":

- No cash-on-delivery confirmation, no cart recovery, no support bot, no "survey". Those words do not appear in the submission.
- Every call happens after the product has arrived.
- The demo shows a system, not a script: who gets called when, what the agent remembers, and the lift against a control group.

---

## 2. The product in one breath

**Dobara** (Hindi for "again") calls a brand's customers at the moment they would lapse, remembers every past conversation, fixes whatever is stopping the next order, and reports the revenue it brought back against a control group.

Alternative names if Dobara is taken: Wapas ("back"), Refill, Ek Aur ("one more").

**Description for the form (under 200 characters):**

> Dobara calls a brand's customers right before they'd lapse, remembers every past call, and brings them back. Live with Cosmix (₹100 Cr ARR); reorder lift measured against a control group.

## 4. The market story, in the order the video tells it

1. **The problem, from inside a brand.** Cosmix spent about 26% of revenue on ads in FY25 to acquire customers. Indian D2C supplement brands typically keep 10% to 30% of them. The retention team is a WhatsApp broadcast nobody reads.
2. **Why voice, why now.** Outbound voice connects 46% to 48% of the time in India, against single-digit engagement on email and muted WhatsApp broadcasts. A four-minute vernacular call now costs under ₹30. Indic speech models crossed usable quality in 2025 to 2026.
3. **The size.** Klaviyo, the email and SMS retention layer for Shopify brands, reported $1.23 billion of revenue in 2025 with over 193,000 customers and 110% net revenue retention. India's brands never got that layer because their customers do not open email. Voice is India's version of it. Estimates of India's D2C market range from $10 billion to $52 billion depending on the source, and nutrition alone is $2 to $2.5 billion; quote the range and the sources, not a single number.
4. **The proof.** Cosmix pilot: calls made, answer rate, reorder lift against control, revenue recovered, top three churn reasons. Early, honest, measured.
5. **The expansion.** The same engine serves anything with a renewal: coaching and nutrition practices (Diet Mojo is lined up), pharma patient-support programs, insurance renewals. Lead with D2C because the buyer is clear and the number is fast.
6. **The business model.** Per reorder recovered (₹50 to ₹100) or per active customer per month, priced against the brand's cost of acquiring a replacement customer.

## 5. Finalist and YC narrative

**Why us.** An operator who has run the margin levers of ₹200 to ₹400 crore consumer brands and now runs operations at one, plus an engineer who shipped at two YC companies. Neither needs the other to explain the problem or the code.

**Why not a Bolna template or SquadStack.** They sell calling minutes with a script. We sell a measured retention lift, own the replenishment model and the customer memory, and hand the brand its churn reasons. The call is the delivery mechanism, not the product.

**Why it gets big.** Every consumer brand's retention team becomes an agent that knows each customer by name. Klaviyo built a billion-dollar company on email for the same brands; voice is the channel that works in India.

## 6. Demo on the day

Two minutes, on stage, live: pick a Cosmix customer who agreed, show their timeline (ordered 1 kg mocha, 28 days ago, said the sweetness was high last call), place the call, let the room hear the agent remember that, handle "I still have some left", and send the reorder link. Then the dashboard: this week's lift against control. Backup recording ready.

## 7. What this changes in the six-day plan

- The call asks two open questions (what would you change; why haven't you reordered) inside the service call. No separate "survey" agent.
- The run-out date model replaces a fixed day-21 trigger; use pack size and servings per day from the order.
- The results page shows lift and cost per reorder, not just call counts.
- The fallback is the same engine on Diet Mojo's clients. Not COD or cart calls, which are templates.
